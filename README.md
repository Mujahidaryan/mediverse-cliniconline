## 🎯 The Problem

Pakistan has 1 doctor per 1,000 patients — WHO recommends 1 per 500.
Rural areas face acute shortages. Specialist access requires travel to major cities,
long queues, and high informal fees. Most patients give up before seeing a specialist.

---

## 💡 The Solution

MediVerse connects patients directly with verified specialists through online
video consultations and home visits — no queue, no travel, transparent pricing
starting at PKR 500.

---

## ✨ Features

- **50+ verified doctors** across 12 specializations
- **Bilingual UI** — English and Urdu throughout
- **Online consultations** — video call from anywhere in Pakistan
- **Home visits** — doctor comes to you
- **Transparent pricing** — no hidden fees, starting PKR 500/session
- **PMDC compliance** — all doctors verified with Pakistan Medical & Dental Council
- **Appointment booking** — real-time slot selection and confirmation
- **Patient history** — all consultations saved and accessible
- **Doctor portal** — separate login and dashboard for practitioners
